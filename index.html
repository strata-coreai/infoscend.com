Who it changes things for

What changes for the people who run distribution.

Software either fits the work or fights it. We designed each role's view around the question that person asks at their first coffee on Monday morning.

Persona 01 · your first user

The Founder / GM

You own the country P&L. Monday morning starts with one question: am I going to hit my quarter? You worry about competitive pressure you can't see, distributor relationships eroding, and questions you can't answer fast enough. You need one screen that compresses fourteen dashboards into the five things that actually matter — with AI explaining the why, not just the what.

What changes
  • One-click drill from dashboard → Distributor 360 → approvals → tactical move, with full audit trail
  • Strategic alerts ordered for an owner's mindset: performance gap, distributor decline, competitive threat
  • AI commentary in board-meeting voice — two sentences max, names dragging distributors
  • Whitespace finder surfaces uncovered Tier-2 outlets matching your category strength
Persona 02 · your right hand

The Head of Sales

You own primary sales. Every distributor is a relationship and a number. The day starts with: who's lagging and why? You worry about a distributor missing target three months running, reps leaving for competitors, pipeline thinning faster than the team can replenish. You need a heatmap that surfaces problems before they become incidents.

What changes
  • Distributor heatmap — every brand × every distributor's pace, on one screen
  • Sales pipeline visualises prospect → active → at-risk → churned, drag-to-move
  • People dashboard surfaces underperforming reps by productive call rate, before review meetings
  • JBP attainment cascade — country target → distributor target → rep daily quota
Persona 03 · the bridge

The Distributor Lead

You own one to three distributors deeply. You drive to the warehouse, ride along on routes, audit displays in person, coach reps. You're the bridge between brand strategy and field execution. What worries you is execution drift — a competitor product creeping into displays, beat plans being skipped, planograms decaying. You need a daily view of every rep's location, every outlet's last audit score, every compliance gap.

What changes
  • Live map of every rep location, every outlet visit status, drift surfaced in real time
  • AI WhatsApp follow-ups to dormant outlets in the local language
  • Distributor compliance — JBP discussion decks generated in BI on demand
  • Beat compliance tracked by rep / district / week — no spreadsheet reconciliation
  • Planogram audits — rep photo gets AI-scored against your reference template
Persona 04 · multi-brand portfolios

The Head of Brand

You manage equity across multiple brands and categories. Your workday is half upstream signal-watching (competitor moves, share of voice, pricing) and half downstream creative production. What keeps you up is a competitor outflanking you with a positioning move, a campaign that ran without measurable lift, a launch that fell flat at retail. You need closed-loop attribution from intel → creative → publish → causal sell-through, per brand, separately.

What changes
  • Competitive intel pipeline — rep photo → AI extraction → price index update → counter-campaign
  • Price compliance — selling at target or is the market eroding price
  • AI Design Studio generates branded creative grounded in your brand DNA
  • Per-brand campaign workspaces — every brand isolated, no cross-contamination
  • Closed-loop attribution — campaign → outlets exposed → sell-through lift, measured per brand
